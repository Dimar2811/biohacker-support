Privacy · Updated September 13, 2026

Your data in Biohacker.

This notice describes the current prerelease app and this website. The operator is Marc Hammerschmidt.

Your personal journal on iPhone

Your stacks, amounts, schedules, intake events and observations are kept in local app storage. The journal is not automatically uploaded to a coach or the community. Device backups may contain this data. Exported files are stored at the destination you choose.

Apple sign-in and subscriptions

Apple handles sign-in and purchases. The app processes identifiers and purchase entitlements needed for authentication and access checks. Separate sign-in for community and coaching uses Apple identity tokens and server-side authentication. Identity information supplied by Apple, including an email address or private relay address, may be processed in the authentication system. StoreKit does not provide Biohacker with payment card numbers.

Community, contacts and private sharing

These separately enabled features use a server running Supabase software. Data includes profile names, handles, roles and user identifiers, contact and coaching connections, invitations, explicitly shared stack copies, community posts, comments and reports. Shared stacks may contain health-related information. Public posts are visible to other community members; private shares are addressed to selected recipients.

Notifications and security

For enabled push features, an APNs device token is associated with the account and used for notifications through Apple. Technical connection data arises from server requests. The app includes no advertising, analytics or AI services and uses no advertising tracking.

Purposes and recipients

Processing supports the features you use, Pro access verification, selected sharing, security and moderation. Recipients include selected users or the community for public posts, Apple and the infrastructure used to operate the server. Detailed operator and hosting documentation will be completed before public launch.

Retention and deletion: current status

Local data, exports, device backups and server data are separate. Community deletion removes or anonymizes community content, but does not currently fully remove the separate authentication identity, all coaching and contact connections, or push data. Complete Apple token revocation is not yet implemented. A final retention schedule for server data and backups has not been confirmed. We do not claim complete or immediate account deletion.

Your rights and contact

Depending on applicable privacy law, rights may include access, correction, erasure, restriction, portability, objection, withdrawal of consent and a complaint to a competent supervisory authority. Send requests to the email or postal address below. Final legal-basis mapping and hosting and retention details will be completed before public launch. Do not post personal access or deletion requests in public GitHub issues.

This website on GitHub Pages

This static website adds no analytics tools, advertising cookies or externally hosted fonts. GitHub Pages logs visitor IP addresses for security purposes. External sites have their own privacy notices. GitHub Privacy Statement · Apple Privacy Policy.

The app has not been publicly released. This factual notice does not certify completion of legal or technical release requirements. The open items above remain to be resolved before public launch.
