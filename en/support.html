Help with Biohacker

How can we help?

Answers about your journal, Pro access and your data.

My Pro access is not recognized

Use the Apple account used for your purchase. Open the Pro screen and choose Restore Purchases. An internet connection is required for verification.

Manage or cancel a subscription

On your iPhone, open Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Biohacker. Deleting the app or a community profile does not cancel a subscription. Apple's cancellation instructions.

Export your journal

Use the app's data export to save a JSON or CSV file. Existing journal export remains available after Pro access expires. Exports may contain sensitive information; choose their destination carefully.

Remove data

Personal journal and community data are separate. Local removal does not delete server data. The current community deletion feature removes or anonymizes community content, but does not yet fully remove the separate authentication identity and all contact, coaching and push data. Complete account deletion still needs to be finished before public launch.

Report a technical issue

Operator: Marc Hammerschmidt. Use the associated GitHub repository for general technical questions. Include iOS and app versions, expected behavior and steps to reproduce.

GitHub issues are public. Do not include health data, journal exports, credentials or personal account details. This channel is for general technical questions. For private requests use support@3d-voxelia.com. Initially include only the information necessary to handle your request.

Private support requests

Email Biohacker support. Never send passwords or Apple identity tokens. Use this private channel for account and privacy requests.

Medical questions

App support does not provide medical advice. Contact a qualified healthcare professional for health-related questions.
